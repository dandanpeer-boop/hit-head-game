Why a Shithead app?

Anyone who has played Shithead knows the argument at the start: which cards burn the pile, which ones reverse, what happens with a 2. Hit Head plays one fixed rule set with its own special cards, so the table agrees before the first card is down. You also never need a full deck, and you never need to find three other players: bots fill the empty seats.

What you get in Hit Head

  • Single-player against bots. It works offline.
  • Online multiplayer. Live games against real players.
  • Friends tables. Open a table, share the invite code, and play with people you know.
  • Tournament mode. Work your way through the Bronze, Silver and Gold tables to win the cup.
  • Global Top 20 leaderboard. Win games, earn coins and climb the ranks.
  • Game store. Spend your coins on profile avatars.

How a game works

Each player gets three hidden cards, three face-up cards and three cards in hand. You play a card equal to or higher than the top of the pile, or you pick the pile up. Empty your hand, then your face-up cards, and finally take your chances on the hidden ones. The last player holding cards loses. Read the complete rules.

Questions

What is Shithead?

Shithead is a card game where everyone tries to get rid of all their cards, and the last player still holding cards is the shithead. It is also called Palace, Karma, Shed and many other names, and the house rules change from group to group.

Is Hit Head the same as Shithead?

Hit Head is a Shithead-style game (the Palace and Karma family) with one fixed rule set and its own special cards: Zero, Transparent, Bomb and the Hit Head card. The full rules are on the how-to-play page, so you can check them before you play with a group used to different house rules.

Is Hit Head free?

Yes. Hit Head is free to download and play. It contains third-party ads and optional in-app purchases.

Can I play Hit Head on an iPhone?

No. Hit Head is available for Android only. On an iPhone you can play Shithead in a web browser or with another app.

Can I play without an internet connection?

Yes. Single-player mode against bots works offline. Multiplayer and friends tables need a connection.

Can I play with my friends?

Yes. Open a friends table, share the invite code, and your friends join your table. A table seats up to four players, and bots fill empty seats.
