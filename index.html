Rules

Simple to learn. Hard to master.

The core rule is one sentence. Everything else is tactics.

1

Play a card equal to or higher

Each player plays a card that matches or beats the previous one. Stack identical cards for bonus effects.

2

Can't play? Take the pile

No valid card? You take the entire discard pile into your hand. The next player starts fresh.

3

Use special cards wisely

Bombs clear the pile. Jokers redirect it. Reset zeros everything. Timing is everything.

4

Empty your hand to win

Clear your hand cards, then your revealed cards, then your hidden ones. Last player holding cards loses.
